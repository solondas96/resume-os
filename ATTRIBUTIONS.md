# Data attributions

ResumeLabs uses the following open data. The generated file `public/data/skills.json` records the same attributions in its `sources` field.

## O*NET 29.1 Database

Skill, knowledge, technology, tool, occupation and alternate-title names. Detailed work activity (DWA) titles, and occupation codes for tagging: each skill is tagged with the three most common O*NET major groups. Task statements are used only to link occupations to DWAs; their text is not stored.

Based on O*NET 29.1 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html. The O*NET data is not endorsed by USDOL/ETA.

## ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations), when included

Skill, knowledge and occupation labels and alternative labels.

ESCO, European Commission. Used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/use-esco/download

## Curated supplement

Technology names and common synonyms written for this project (`src/intelligence/skills.ts`). Project-authored, under the project's licence.

## Field synonym supplements

Skill names and resume synonyms for 12 non-tech fields, about 30 each (`src/intelligence/fieldSynonyms.ts`). Written for this project, not taken from a licensed dataset, under the project's licence.
